# AI School Assessment Platform

# Business Process Flow

## Purpose

The AI School Assessment Platform automates the complete assessment process in educational institutions using Artificial Intelligence.

The platform supports three user roles:

- Administrator
- Teacher
- Student

Artificial Intelligence assists teachers by automatically evaluating submitted assignments.

---

# Business Workflow

```text
Administrator

↓

Creates Teachers

↓

Creates Students

↓

Creates Subjects

↓

Teacher

↓

Creates Assignment

↓

Publishes Assignment

↓

Student

↓

Views Assignment

↓

Uploads Homework

↓

Submission

↓

AI Review

↓

Teacher Review

↓

Final Grade

↓

Student Views Result
```

---

# Administrator Responsibilities

The administrator manages the educational platform.

Functions:

- Manage teachers
- Manage students
- Manage subjects
- Monitor system usage

---

# Teacher Responsibilities

Teacher creates educational content.

Functions:

- Create assignments
- Review submissions
- View AI recommendations
- Approve or edit grades
- Publish final grades

---

# Student Responsibilities

Student completes assignments.

Functions:

- View assignments
- Upload homework
- View AI feedback
- View teacher feedback
- Track learning progress

---

# AI Responsibilities

Artificial Intelligence evaluates submitted work.

Functions:

- Read uploaded files
- Analyze solutions
- Generate score
- Generate feedback
- Identify strengths
- Identify weaknesses
- Recommend improvements
